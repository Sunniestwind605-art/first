Using an iPhone or iPad?

Install Campus Eats to the Home Screen before enabling runner notifications.

  1. Open this page directly in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Campus Eats from the Home Screen icon.
  5. Return to the staff portal, select your name and enable notifications.
