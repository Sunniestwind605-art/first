Order alerts

Campus Eats can send free browser notifications when a runner accepts your order and whenever its delivery status changes.

Recommended: enable notifications on the phone or computer you normally use for Campus Eats. You can still track orders on the website if you skip this.
Using an iPhone or iPad?

Apple requires Campus Eats to be opened as a Home Screen web app before it can send background notifications.

  1. Open this page directly in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Campus Eats from the new Home Screen icon.
  5. Return to Notifications and tap Enable notifications.
